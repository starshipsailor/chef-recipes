Grilled Prawn Skewers

Serves 2 · Prep 10 min · Cook 6 min

Ingredients

  • prawns300 g
  • garlic2 cloves
  • lemon juice1 tbsp
  • green chilli1
  • paprika1 tsp
  • olive oil1 tbsp

Method

  1. Toss the prawns with garlic, lemon, paprika, chilli and oil.
  2. Thread onto skewers.
  3. Griddle 2-3 min per side until pink and lightly charred.
  4. Finish with coriander and a squeeze of lemon.
Tip. Don't overcook - prawns turn rubbery fast.
