Chana Masala (chickpea curry)

Serves 2 · Prep 10 min · Cook 30 min

Ingredients

  • chickpeas1 tin (400 g)
  • onion1
  • tomato2
  • ginger-garlic paste1 tbsp
  • green chilli1
  • cumin1 tsp
  • coriander powder1 tsp
  • garam masala1/2 tsp
  • amchur1/2 tsp
  • turmeric1/4 tsp

Method

  1. Fry the onion in minimal oil till golden; add ginger-garlic and chilli.
  2. Add chopped tomato and the dry spices; cook till jammy.
  3. Add the chickpeas and a splash of water; simmer 15 min.
  4. Finish with amchur, garam masala and coriander.
Tip. Mash a few chickpeas to thicken the sauce.
