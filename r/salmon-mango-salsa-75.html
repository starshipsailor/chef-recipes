Salmon + Mango Salsa

Serves 2 · Prep 10 min · Cook 8 min

Ingredients

  • salmon fillet2
  • ripe mango1
  • red onion1/4
  • lime1
  • jalapeno1/2
  • coriandersmall handful
  • olive oil1 tbsp

Method

  1. Dice the mango, red onion, jalapeno and coriander; toss with lime juice and a little oil.
  2. Season the salmon and pan-sear skin-side down 3-4 min until crisp.
  3. Flip and cook 2 min more until just done.
  4. Top with the mango salsa.
Tip. Let the salsa sit 10 min so the flavours mingle.
