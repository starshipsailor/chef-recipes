Thai Basil Chicken (Pad Krapow, lean)

Serves 2 · Prep 8 min · Cook 10 min

Ingredients

  • chicken mince400 g
  • thai basillarge handful
  • garlic3 cloves
  • birds eye chilli1-2
  • green beans100 g
  • soy sauce (low salt)1 tbsp
  • fish sauce2 tsp

Method

  1. Stir-fry the garlic and chilli in a little oil for 30 sec.
  2. Add the chicken mince and brown, breaking it up.
  3. Add green beans, soy and fish sauce; toss 2-3 min until cooked.
  4. Off the heat, fold through the Thai basil until wilted.
  5. Serve over rice with a fried egg if you like.
Tip. Keep the heat high so it fries rather than stews.
