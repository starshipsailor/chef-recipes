Roasted Veg & Butterbean Mash Bowl

Serves 4 · Prep 10 min · Cook 25 min

Ingredients

  • butterbeans, drained2 cans
  • courgette, chunked1
  • bell pepper, chunked1
  • cherry tomatoes200 g
  • red onion, wedged1
  • garlic, crushed2 cloves
  • rocket2 handfuls
  • olive oil2 tbsp
  • lemon1
  • black pepperto taste
  • saltto taste

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200C.
  2. Toss the courgette, bell pepper, cherry tomatoes and red onion with 1 tbsp olive oil, salt and pepper.
  3. Spread on a tray and roast 20-25 minutes until charred at the edges.
  4. Meanwhile, warm the butterbeans in a pan with the crushed garlic, a splash of water and 1 tbsp olive oil.
  5. Mash the beans roughly to a creamy base; season with salt, pepper and a squeeze of lemon.
  6. Spoon the butterbean mash into bowls.
  7. Pile the roasted vegetables over the mash.
  8. Top with rocket, a drizzle of olive oil and a final squeeze of lemon.
Tip. A pinch of chilli flakes or smoked paprika on the beans lifts it. Swap in whatever veg needs using up.
