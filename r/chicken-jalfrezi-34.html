Chicken Jalfrezi

Serves 4 · Prep 15 min · Cook 25 min

Ingredients

  • chicken breast, cut into strips500 g
  • bell peppers, thick sliced2
  • onion, sliced1 large
  • tomato, chopped1
  • ginger-garlic paste1 tbsp
  • green chilli, slit1-2
  • cumin1 tsp
  • coriander powder1 tsp
  • garam masala1/2 tsp
  • turmeric1/2 tsp
  • oil1 tbsp
  • saltto taste

Method

  1. Heat half the oil in a wide pan on high; sear the chicken strips until just cooked and lightly browned. Remove and set aside.
  2. Add the rest of the oil; saute the sliced onion until softened.
  3. Stir in the ginger-garlic paste and green chilli; cook 1 minute.
  4. Add cumin, coriander powder and turmeric; stir 30 seconds.
  5. Add the tomato and cook to a thick masala, 4-5 minutes.
  6. Add the bell peppers and stir-fry 2-3 minutes so they stay crunchy.
  7. Return the chicken; add salt and toss to coat.
  8. Sprinkle the garam masala, toss 2 minutes, and serve hot with roti or rice.
Tip. Keep the heat high and peppers crunchy - jalfrezi is a dry, vibrant stir-fry, not a gravy. Don't overcook the chicken.
