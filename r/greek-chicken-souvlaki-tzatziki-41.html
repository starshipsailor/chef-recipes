Greek Chicken Souvlaki + Tzatziki

Serves 2 · Prep 15 min · Cook 12 min

Ingredients

  • chicken breast400 g
  • greek yoghurt150 g
  • lemon juice1 tbsp
  • garlic2 cloves
  • dried oregano1 tsp
  • olive oil1 tbsp
  • cucumber1/2
  • dillsmall handful

Method

  1. Whisk half the yoghurt with lemon, garlic, oregano and oil; marinate the diced chicken 15 min+.
  2. Thread onto skewers and griddle 8-10 min, turning, until charred and cooked.
  3. Grate the cucumber, squeeze dry, and stir into the rest of the yoghurt with dill and a little garlic.
  4. Serve skewers with the tzatziki.
Tip. Longer marinating deepens the flavour.
